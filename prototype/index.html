03 — Publications

Research papers

Work I’ve written up, presented, or contributed to.

2025 Preprint · arXiv

Low-Rank Adaptation Under Aggressive Post-Training Quantisation

We study how LoRA adapters trained in half precision degrade once the base model is quantised to four bits, and show that adapter placement matters more than adapter rank for retaining task performance on code-reasoning benchmarks.

  • Quantisation
  • PEFT
  • Evaluation
2025 Workshop paper

Pedagogical Guardrails for Code-Generating Tutors

An LLM tutor that simply hands over the answer teaches nothing. We describe a decoding-time intervention that steers a model toward Socratic hints, and measure the trade-off against raw task completion.

  • AI in education
  • Decoding
  • Human evaluation
2024 Technical report

Throughput Characteristics of Batched Inference on Consumer GPUs

A measurement study of continuous batching across memory-constrained hardware, with practical guidance on picking concurrency limits before the KV cache becomes the bottleneck.

  • Systems
  • vLLM
  • Benchmarking
